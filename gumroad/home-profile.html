And you are the yellow knot in the middle, watching yourself not do the thing. Again.

  • You know exactly what to do, and you physically can't start.
  • You make the most detailed plan, and it falls apart before lunch.
  • You know why you should stop, and you reach for it anyway.
  • You're not lazy. Two strong forces are pulling you in opposite directions.
The idea

You can't force either side. You can broker a deal.

You can't make your ADHD brain follow the plan. You can't logic your autistic brain out of needing certainty. But your thinking brain can sit both of them down and negotiate something small enough that both say yes.

Emotional problems need emotional solutions.The Peace Treaty doesn't argue with your feelings. It bargains with them, the way a kid trades lunch, until the plan feels like their own idea.
