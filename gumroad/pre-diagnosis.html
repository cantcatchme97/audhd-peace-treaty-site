Why the usual tests leave you confused

Every test checks one side

ADHD checklists ask about ADHD. Autism checklists ask about autism. Nobody asks about the fight between them.

And when ADHD and autism live in one head, they hide each other. The ADHD mess covers the autistic need for order. The autistic routines cover the ADHD chaos. So you score "a bit of both" on everything and walk away with nothing.

That's what this map is for.It asks about both sides with the same questions, side by side, and then asks about the tug of war in the middle. That's the part most people with AuDHD have never had words for.

What "pre-diagnosis" means

It comes before a diagnosis, never instead of one.

This map can't diagnose you. Only a qualified clinician can diagnose ADHD or autism. What it can do is give you clear words and real examples, so you walk into an assessment knowing what to say. Or, if an assessment isn't possible for you right now, so you finally understand your own brain anyway.
